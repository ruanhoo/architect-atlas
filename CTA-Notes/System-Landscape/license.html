The free edition cannot access any CRM objects, but the sharing model entities still exist and activate when a module is enabled.

Standard Salesforce License

Most standard Salesforce license types take full advantage of the sharing model components. The license may restrict access to certain modules or objects, but the sharing infrastructure still exists.

High Volume Customer Portal (HVCP)

  • Does NOT use the standard sharing model
  • Sharing works by foreign key match between the portal user (account/contact ID) and records
  • HVCP license is only for Customer Portal — NOT Partner Portal
  • High-volume users have no roles → no role hierarchy performance overhead

Chatter Free License

  • Collaboration-only; does NOT follow the standard sharing model
  • Features: Chatter, Profile, People, Groups, Files, Chatter Messenger
  • No access to CRM records (standard or custom objects) or Content functionality
  • No sharing model participation

Community License FAQs

Do I need community licenses to create Experience Cloud sites?

  • Enterprise, Performance, Unlimited: up to 100 sites can be created without buying community licenses
  • Exception: Partner Central template requires at least one Partner Community license
  • Legacy portal licenses can be used for sites created with Experience Cloud

Are community licenses user-bound or site-bound?

  • Community licenses are associated with users, not a specific site
  • Supports all internal and portal licenses, including legacy Customer Portal and Authenticated Website

Login-Based vs Member-Based Licenses

Member-BasedLogin-Based
Billing unitNamed seatMonthly logins purchased
How consumedPer provisioned user1 login per user per day
Multiple logins same dayN/AStill 1 login consumed
Site switchingN/ANo additional consumption
  • Login overages: calculated over 12-month period from contract start; entitlements roll over month to month
  • Monitor: LoginHistory table; Usage-based Entitlements list (Setup → Company Information)
  • Usage-based entitlement resources: Customer Community Logins, Power Customer Community Logins, Partner Community Logins, External Apps Logins (and Daily Unique variants)

Channel Licenses

  • Optimised for partners; buy a specific number for your partner organisation
  • Each partner account with an assigned license gets up to 40 partner users (expandable)

Guest Users

  • Do NOT consume external licenses
  • Page view limits by edition (e.g. Enterprise: 6M/year); overages calculated annually

Salesforce Shield (3 components)

  1. Shield Platform Encryption — encrypts sensitive data at rest; AES-256; customer-controlled key management
  2. Real-Time Event Monitoring — streams events as platform events; stores in big objects; near-real-time visibility
  3. Field Audit Trail — retains field history up to 10 years; built on big data backend
